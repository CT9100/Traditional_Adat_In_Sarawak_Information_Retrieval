# Selected Traditional Adat in Sarawak Information Retrieval

This project solves the difficulty of finding accurate and relevant information about selected traditional Adat in Sarawak for students, researchers, and the public who want to understand and preserve local cultural knowledge.

## Problem statement
 Traditional Adat from various communities in Sarawak contains valuable cultural knowledge that is important to preserve for future generations. 
 However, information about these customs may be scattered across different sources, making it difficult for students, researchers, and the public to find relevant information easily. This can limit people's understanding of traditional practices and their cultural significance. 
 Therefore, an information retrieval system is needed to provide a more organised and accessible way to search for information about selected traditional Adat in Sarawak.
