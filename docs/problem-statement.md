# Problem Statement

## Who is affected?
Students, researchers, educators, and the public who want 
to learn about traditional Adat from various communities in Sarawak.

## What exactly is going wrong?
Information about traditional Adat is scattered across 
different sources, making it difficult to find relevant and
reliable information quickly.

## Why does it matter?
It makes cultural knowledge harder to access, understand 
and preserve for future generations.

## Problem statement (3 to 5 sentences)
Traditional Adat from various communities in Sarawak contains 
valuable cultural knowledge that needs to be preserved. 
However, students, researchers, educators and the public may 
find it difficult to access relevant information because it 
is scattered across different sources. This makes searching for 
and understanding traditional customs time-consuming and less convenient. 
Therefore, an information retrieval system is needed to organise 
and improve access to information about selected traditional Adat in Sarawak.
